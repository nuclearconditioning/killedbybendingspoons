The team

129 people went in February 2023, then nearly everyone else by July. The US and Chile offices closed and the whole operation moved to Europe. Effectively nobody who built Evernote works on Evernote.

The product

The free plan was cut to 50 notes and one notebook. The Pro price went from $37 a year to $250 a year over three years. The engineering rebuild was substantial: the Java monolith was migrated off 750 hand-provisioned VMs in about six months. Follow the Money reported users describing sync failures, slow starts and crashes, and users moving to competitors.
