The team

The entire FiLMiC Inc. team was laid off in December 2023, roughly fifteen months after the deal. The people who had built the app for over a decade were gone in one round.

The product

Still on the App Store. The one-off purchase model became a subscription. Beyond that change and the December 2023 layoffs, no reporting on the app's development was found.
