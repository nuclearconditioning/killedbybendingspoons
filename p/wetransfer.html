The team

75% of a workforce of more than 350 was cut, leaving roughly 88 people. Luca Ferrari's public framing: "the vision we developed is of a smaller, more sharply focused WeTransfer organization." The Amsterdam design culture and the WePresent editorial arm shrank with it.

The product

The no-account free transfer still exists but was capped at ten transfers a month in December 2024. One documented user's plan went from €10 to €23 a month, a rise of about 130%.
